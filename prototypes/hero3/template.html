What you will learn

From your first measurement to your own lehenga.

01

Measure

Where the tape goes, how snug to pull it, and what each number means.

02

Draft the pattern

Your numbers become a paper pattern, drawn with a ruler and pencil.

03

Stitch

Hours on an industrial machine, until your seams run straight on their own.

04

Your first blouse

A fitted blouse, darted, piped and closed with hooks you set yourself.

05

Lehenga

A flared lehenga, gathered at the waist and tied with a dori and tassels.
