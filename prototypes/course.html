The course

What you'll learn, module by module.

Both courses run in the studio on industrial machines. The 6-month course adds more drafts, aari work, boutique management and a local market visit.

6 modules

  1. Pattern making and stitching

    • How to take body measurements
    • Machine practice on an industrial sewing machine
    • Cutting practice
    • Making and stitching cord piping
    • Darts: what they are and how to give them to a garment
    • Drafting the basic bodice block
    • Kurta draftsStraight cut (side and centre slit) · A-line kurti
    • More kurta draftsCentre slit kurta with placket · designer tunic
    • Sleeve draftsRegular · puff (up, down, both) · low sleeves
    • More sleevesPetal · waterfall · roll-on

  2. Blouses

    • One-dart and four-dart blouse, with and without waistband
    • Princess cut, deep and high neckPadded · zipper · hooks
    • Collar blouse · boat neck blouse
    • Katori blouse · shirt collar blouse · cape

  3. Lehenga and gowns

    • LehengaPanel flare · umbrella flare · pleated
    • Fishcut lehenga · knot skirt
    • GownsPanel flare · umbrella flare

  4. Basics of design

    • Elements of design
    • Principles of design
    • Colour theory and composition

  5. Aari work

    • Practice with the aari needle on the frame
    • Aari stylesPearl · beads · zardozi · thread work

  6. Finishing techniques

    • Hooks and eyes, blind hemming, tassel making
    • Bead and crystal hangings

  7. Branding and marketing

    • Building a brand that stands out
    • Why sales matter, and how to sell
    • Building your personal brand
    • Introduction to boutique management
    • Types of fabric, with a local market visit
