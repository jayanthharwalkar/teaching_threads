What you see

The clay sewing machine plays frame by frame as you scroll (the pipeline we already have). The thread leaving the needle becomes a dashed stitch that underlines the headline, then runs down the page into the five stages, ticking each one from grey to black.

Why it works

One signature motion, reusing what's built. The thread becomes the spine of the whole page, so the hero, the stages and the footer feel like one garment.

On mobile

Use a portrait Kling render, pinned for at most 2 screen-heights. Frame 0 is a normal <img> so the page loads fast. A WhatsApp pill sits bottom-right from the first second.

What you see

The hero loads as a sheet of pattern paper: 1 cm grid, grainline notes, a dashed cutting curve. The kainchi runs along the line, the piece lifts away, and the clay dress form is revealed underneath.

Why it works

The biggest 'wow' of the set, and it builds on the kainchi prototype already on this branch. The micro-type on the paper is real copy, so it still explains the offer.

On mobile

Plays once on load, never tied to scroll. People with reduced motion see the after-cut state.

Rules every option keeps

  • Eyebrow, headline, one-line offer and both buttons are plain HTML, visible on load, never waiting on an animation.
  • Primary CTA: WhatsApp with a prefilled message ("Hi, I'm interested in the fashion design course"). Secondary: book a studio visit.
  • Rejected: a zoom through video-filled letters (breaks on 375px phones) and a cursor thread trail (does nothing on touch, so it moved to the founder page).
