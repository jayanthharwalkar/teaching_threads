Can Meshy make a dress form this good?

Mostly yes, and a dress form is close to the easiest thing you could ask it for: one smooth, solid object with no thin parts except the pole. Two things work in our favour. First, our look is plain matte clay-white, so we can throw away Meshy's texture (its weakest part) and light the model with a simple clay material in the browser. Second, the bare form has no fabric folds, which AI 3D still gets wrong.

What to expect it to get wrong: a lumpy or slightly asymmetric torso, a bent or melted stand pole, a soft neck knob, and the draped tape in our still turning into a blob. That's why the tape should be added in code, as it is in the demo, never baked into the model.

How I'd do it

  1. Make a clean input image first: the dress form alone, no tape, front three-quarter view, flat light background. Better still, make front, side and back views with ChatGPT and use Meshy's multi-image-to-3D.
  2. Generate with the newest model (Meshy 6, or 7 if your account shows it). Turn texture off, or ignore it.
  3. Remesh to quads for a smooth surface, about 30-50k faces. Smart Topology also works.
  4. Download as GLB. In Blender: straighten the pole (or replace it with a perfect cylinder), mirror the torso for symmetry, smooth shading, then compress to under 2 MB.
  5. On the site: Google's <model-viewer> or three.js, a still poster image first, 3D loaded after the page is ready.

Cost and licence

  • Image-to-3D is about 20-25 credits a try. Budget 4-6 tries for a good one.
  • The free plan (100 credits a month) is licensed CC BY 4.0, so you'd need to credit Meshy. Pro ($20/month) gives full commercial use. One month of Pro is enough for this.
  • Check current numbers on meshy.ai/pricing; third-party sites disagree.

Speed and SEO

  • three.js is about 150 KB plus the model. With a poster image as the first thing on screen, Google sees a normal fast page; the 3D is a bonus on top.

Meshy prompt (if you use text or need to describe the image)

A professional tailor's dress form, female torso with natural bust, waist and hips, smooth matte clay-white surface, a small round knob at the neck, mounted on a single straight vertical pole with a flat round base. Perfectly symmetrical, clean smooth surfaces, no fabric, no tape, no seams, no text. Studio product model, realistic proportions, not cartoon, not stylised.

Negative / avoid: cartoon, toy, chibi, stylised, exaggerated proportions, mannequin head, arms, legs, clothing, tape measure, bent pole, texture noise.
