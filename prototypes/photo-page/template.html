Her courses work the same way. You learn at the machine, on real garments.

What you'll learn

From your first measurement to your own lehenga.

  1. Measure

    Take body measurements the way a tailor does, and note the numbers a pattern needs.

  2. Draft the pattern

    Turn those numbers into a bodice block with a ruler and pencil.

  3. Stitch

    Practise on an industrial machine until your lines run straight and even.

  4. Your first blouse

    A fitted blouse with darts, piping at the neck and hooks you set yourself.

  5. Lehenga

    Draft, stitch and finish a flared lehenga with a dori and tassels.

Two courses

Choose how deep you go.

3months

Measuring, pattern drafting and machine stitching. Kurtas, sleeves, blouses, lehengas and gowns. Finishing, the basics of design, and how to brand and sell your work.

6months

Everything in the 3-month course, plus more drafts like the katori blouse and fishcut lehenga, aari work with beads and zardozi, boutique management, and a visit to the local fabric market.

Both courses run in the studio on industrial machines. Ask on WhatsApp for fees and the next batch date.
