# Nano Banana prompts

Direction: `course-visual-direction.md`. POV shots of clay-white objects on a flat page-coloured backdrop (#F3F1EC), no room, no shadows, whip pans between scenes.

Settings for every frame: **9:16 vertical**, highest resolution available. Copy each prompt as one block.

---

## Week 1: Take a measurement (dress form)

```
First-person POV, vertical 9:16. We stand very close to a dressmaker's dress form, slightly above bust height, looking at it. Two smooth clay-white sculpted hands come in from the bottom of the frame and hold a measuring tape with black markings around the form's bust, about to pull it snug.

Everything is a minimal 3D clay render in matte warm off-white (#F3F1EC): the dress form, its stand and the hands. Smooth surfaces, no fabric texture, no wood, no metal shine. The only black is the tape's markings.

The background is an endless flat field of exactly #F3F1EC, perfectly even. No room, no walls, no floor, no horizon, no cast shadows, no contact shadows, no vignette. Soft, even light from the upper left gives gentle shading on the objects themselves so they still look 3D. Calm and quiet. No text, no logos, no real skin.
```

## Fixes if it goes wrong

- **Hands look odd** (extra fingers, wrong angle): add "two hands, five fingers each, seen from the person's own point of view".
- **Real skin instead of clay:** add "the hands are sculpted from the same white clay as everything else, like a white mannequin's hands".
- **A room, floor or shadow appears:** add "objects float in an empty seamless void, like a product render on a plain backdrop".
- **Background too grey or yellow:** add "the background must be exactly #F3F1EC, perfectly even". A small colour difference is fine; it can be corrected in code.
- **Not POV** (camera sees the object from across the room): add "very close, as if through the eyes of the person doing the work".
- **Colour or metal creeping in:** add "monochrome off-white, no colour except black on the named parts".

## Tips

- Generate 2-3 versions of each and keep the one with the flattest background and the best hands.
- Keep the light from the upper left in every scene so they read as one set.
- If the hands won't come out right, generate the same scene without hands as a fallback. A still without hands still works for the scroll-and-whip-pan version.
